in shape and recoloured onto this page's tokens; its three banding marks are dropped (this page has no shading).
scripts/icons.py does the conversion and writes assets/symbols.svg.
ATP is a shaded colour illustration (yellow phosphates, grey sugar) and collapses into one dark blob when
flattened to this page's single ink, so Figure 2 draws ATP in the page's own hand instead; the symbol is
still built and left unused. Each symbol's viewBox is set to the drawing's own bounds, measured with
getBBox() in the browser: the files' declared viewBoxes are larger than what they draw.
