What it is

Datum Line is a shared map of every lab’s control cells. A lab uploads the control images it already takes and sees where its cells sit among every lab running the same cell line.

The name

A datum line is the line a surveyor measures from: mean sea level on a map. Datum is Latin for “a thing given”, and the singular of data. In cell biology the control is that line, since every result is a difference from it, and today each lab draws its own. Datum Line draws one for the field. The line is also the cell line.
